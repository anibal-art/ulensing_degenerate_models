Event 577615 H0 tE-bound convergence test

The H0 optimum follows the upper tE bound for:
  5000 d
  20000 d
  50000 d
  100000 d

With tE_max = 500000 d, the optimum becomes interior:
  tE = 140884.93 d
  chi2 = 33905.480289

With tE_max = 1000000 d:
  tE = 142852.71 d
  chi2 = 33905.476495

Difference:
  chi2(500000) - chi2(1000000) = 0.003794

This is well below the adopted convergence tolerance Delta chi2 = 0.1.

Conclusion:
The event has a finite very-long-timescale H0 pseudo-solution.
A fixed tE_max = 5000 d is not universally safe for H0.
Use a standard domain followed by automatic tE-bound escalation when
the H0 winner is near the upper tE boundary.

This test concerns H0/shared nuisance bounds only. H1 optimization and
parallax bounds require independent validation.
